Welcome to the official website of Ravjee Detergent (“we”, “our”, “us”).

We value your trust and take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains, in detail, how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you visit our website https://www.ravjeedetergent.com/. This website is a frontend-only informational website. We do not operate user accounts, online purchases, or cookies. We only collect information that you voluntarily provide to us.

By using this Website, you accept the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1 Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • The Ravjee Detergent website and any subpages
  • Contact interactions initiated by visitors
  • Basic information provided voluntarily by users

This policy does not apply to third-party websites linked from our site or external platforms.

2 Information We Collect

We collect information in two primary ways:

2.1 Information You Voluntarily Provide

You may choose to contact us through:

  • Email
  • Contact form (if implemented)
  • Phone
  • Social media links

If you contact us, you may provide:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Business name (optional)
  • Message, feedback, or inquiry

2.2 Information Not Collected Automatically

Unlike many modern websites, our site does NOT collect:

  • Cookies or tracking files
  • User behavior analytics
  • Google Analytics tracking
  • Advertising pixels
  • Browser details & Location data
  • IP addresses (stored only by host logs outside our control)

3 How We Use the Information You Provide

Any information you voluntarily share with us is used only for:

3.1 Communication

Responding to your inquiries, providing customer support, or addressing business questions.

3.2 Internal Support

Improving our Website content or understanding general types of inquiries received.

3.3 No Marketing Use

We do not use your data for marketing, newsletters, or promotions unless you explicitly request or consent.

4 Information We Do NOT Collect or Store

To maintain full transparency, we confirm that our website does NOT:

Create user accounts
Store passwords
Process payments
Use cookies
Collect analytics
Run advertising scripts

Your visit remains anonymous unless you choose to contact us.

5 Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal information to any third party.

We share information only in the following cases:

  • 5.1 To Respond to Your Inquiry: Our team may share your message internally to provide appropriate support.
  • 5.2 Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or government authority.
  • 5.3 Hosting Provider Logs: Basic server logs (managed by Netlify or Hostinger) may exist for security purposes. These logs are not controlled, accessed, or used by us.

6 Data Security

We take privacy and confidentiality very seriously. Even though we do not store sensitive information, we follow industry-standard measures:

  • Secure hosting environment
  • SSL (HTTPS) website encryption
  • Restricted access to emails and inquiries
  • No unnecessary data storage

Note: However, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure. If you share information through email or forms, you do so at your own discretion.

7 Data Retention

We retain your voluntarily submitted information only as long as necessary to:

  • Respond to your message
  • Maintain business communication
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not store unnecessary data. If you would like your information deleted, you may request it anytime.

8 Children’s Privacy

Our Website is not intended for children under 13 years of age.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If a child has shared information with us, we will delete it upon request.

9 Third-Party Links

Our Website may contain links to social media (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn) or partner sites. These third-party platforms have their own privacy policies.

We are not responsible for their content, data handling, or security practices.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if website features change, we introduce new communication forms, or legal requirements change.

We will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page to reflect the changes.
